Global Sourcing Partner & Specialized Trading Company — Keep Your World Moving

AT International is a premier global supplier, exporter, and specialized B2B trading partner based in Guangzhou, China. We streamline cross-border procurement for industrial sealing solutions, motorcycle parts, and e-bike components for distributors and commercial buyers in over 40 countries across six continents.

Core Export Product Divisions

  • Industrial Sealing Solutions: Precision Valve Stem Seals (FKM/Viton), Fluororubber and NBR O-Rings, Heavy-Duty Rotary Shaft Oil Seals, and Custom Molded Rubber Seals for automotive, hydraulic, and industrial machinery.
  • Motorcycle Parts: Forged crankshafts, cylinder heads, engine blocks, piston assemblies, clutch plates, and full engine overhaul components manufactured to OEM precision.
  • E-Bike Parts: High-efficiency BLDC hub motors, intelligent motor controllers, lithium-ion battery chargers, and electronic throttles engineered for modern electric mobility.

International Compliance & Quality Standards

All products are sourced in strict compliance with ISO 9001:2015 quality management systems, RoHS environmental standards, and REACH chemical safety regulations with verified third-party inspection records and material traceability.
